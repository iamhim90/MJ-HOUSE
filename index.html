Advance Booking

Ensure the plot is booked well in advance, with a signed agreement outlining the terms.

Payment Schedule

Follow the payment terms, including any deposits, which might be non-refundable. A non-refundable advance payment of 40% is required to confirm the booking.

Start and End Times

Stick to the scheduled event time as per the booking agreement. Late hours might attract additional charges or penalties.

Curfew Compliance

Adhere to local noise curfews to avoid complaints from neighboring properties.

Guest Limit

Do not exceed the venue's capacity for safety reasons.

Fire Safety

Follow fire safety regulations, including having fire extinguishers and no flames unless specified areas are provided.

Noise Cut-off Time

Follow any regulation regarding when music should be turned off or reduced in volume.

Waste Disposal

Properly dispose of all trash. Some venues provide bins - take care of your own waste disposal.

Damage to Property

Any damage to the property must be reported and will typically need to be paid for by the event organizer.

Our Story

A Venue as Elegant
as Your Occasion

MJ Culture is Dahod's premier farmhouse venue — a sanctuary where lush gardens, premium facilities, and warm hospitality combine to create unforgettable celebrations. From intimate gatherings to grand weddings, we craft every event with precision and grace.

Located on Ukardi Road, our venue spans across beautifully manicured grounds, featuring an iconic dolphin fountain, premium indoor halls, and open-air lawns bathed in golden light.
